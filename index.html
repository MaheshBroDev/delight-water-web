About us

A water treatment workshop in Galewela, serving the whole island.

Delight Water Solutions is a Sri Lankan engineering company specialising in reverse osmosis, UV purification, and complete water treatment systems. From a small workshop in Galewela, we have grown into a full installation, maintenance, and 24/7 support operation covering every district in the country.

Whether you need a 5-litre-per-hour domestic purifier under your kitchen sink, or a 50,000-litre-per-day industrial RO plant for a factory or hotel, we design, build, install, and service the entire system ourselves. No middlemen.

  • Multi-stage RO systems from 5 LPH to 50,000 LPH
  • UV purification, water softeners, iron-removal media
  • DM/DI plants, sea water RO, nano RO
  • Professional installation & 24/7 technical support
