# PERMEATE

### An algorithmic philosophy for the Delight Water Solutions stage

---

## I. The movement

**Permeate** is a generative aesthetic built on a single physical truth: *separation is
beautiful*. Not the separation of subtraction — of things removed and discarded — but the
separation of *revelation*, where a chaotic population passes through an invisible law and
emerges sorted into what is pure and what is not. Reverse osmosis is the quiet violence at
the heart of clean water: pressure applied against nature's own tendency to mix, a membrane
that says *yes* to water and *no* to everything else. Permeate takes that membrane and makes
it the governing operator of an entire visual system.

Every particle in this world carries a hidden scalar — its **solute load**. It is invisible.
It cannot be seen directly, only inferred from behaviour. Two particles born a micron apart,
identical in every visible respect, will meet the membrane and diverge forever: one crosses
into stillness and light, one is turned back into the churn. The composition is not designed;
it is *adjudicated*. The artist's hand sets the threshold and then withdraws. What remains on
screen is the emergent census of a million small verdicts — and this is precisely why it must
be a meticulously crafted algorithm, tuned across countless iterations, because a threshold
set carelessly produces noise, while a threshold set by someone at the absolute top of their
field produces inevitability.

## II. Field before form

Nothing in Permeate is animated. Everything is *driven*. Position is never stored and
integrated; it is evaluated — a pure function of a particle's seed and the current time,
resolved entirely in the vertex stage. This constraint is not an optimisation afterthought,
it is the philosophy's spine: a system that can be evaluated at any instant, in any order,
from a single seed, is a system that is *true* rather than merely recorded. Scrub time
backwards and the water un-mixes with perfect fidelity. The same seed on a flagship desktop
and a four-year-old Android renders the identical instant of the identical world, differing
only in how many witnesses are present to observe it.

The motion field itself is curl noise — the curl of a layered simplex potential, which is
divergence-free by construction. Divergence-free means incompressible. Incompressible means
*this behaves like a fluid and not like smoke*, and the difference is legible even to a viewer
who could never name it. Three octaves, each roughly doubling in frequency and halving in
contribution: the first octave carries the slow ocean drift, the second the eddies that make
the eye track, the third the shimmer that survives close inspection. A fourth octave was tried
and discarded — it read as grain, not water. This is the kind of decision that only emerges
from painstaking optimisation, and its absence is the mark of the work.

## III. The membrane as a law, not an object

The membrane is not geometry. It is a scalar field with a gradient, and particles feel only
its gradient. Approaching it, they are compressed — velocity rises, spacing tightens, the
visual density climbs into a bright meniscus of pressure. This is the concentration polarisation
layer that every RO engineer knows and every viewer feels without knowing. At the boundary the
verdict is rendered: solute load below threshold passes, and in passing the particle is
*transfigured* — it loses its turbulence, cools toward the palest aqua, and drifts into laminar
order. Above threshold it is rejected, swept tangentially along the surface into the brine
channel, where it darkens and accelerates and eventually leaves the frame.

The recovery ratio — the fraction that permeates — is the single most important number in the
composition, exactly as it is the single most important number in a real plant. Set it too high
and the image becomes an undifferentiated glow with nothing at stake. Too low and the membrane
reads as a wall rather than a filter. The correct value sits near the real-world figure for a
well-designed brackish system, and it was found the way every good parameter is found: by
building the whole thing, watching it for a long time, and adjusting by hundredths.

## IV. Light as evidence

Colour in Permeate is never decorative and never assigned — it is *measured*. Hue reports
solute load. Luminance reports velocity. Opacity reports depth below the surface. A viewer
scanning the frame is reading live instrumentation and mistaking it for atmosphere, which is
the highest compliment a data-driven image can receive. The palette is drawn from water seen
honestly: the near-black of a deep tank, the sodium-lit teal of a plant at night, and one
single hot aqua reserved exclusively for permeate — the only truly bright thing in the world,
so that purity is the brightest fact on screen and the eye is drawn to it involuntarily.

Above everything, caustics. Not a texture, not a loop, but the genuine article: the ridged
interference of two counter-propagating wave systems, sharpened by a power curve until the
thin bright filaments emerge on their own. Caustics are the shadow of a surface, and rendering
them from the same wave function that displaces the surface itself — rather than faking them
with a scrolling map — is the sort of internal consistency that costs hours and reads, to the
casual eye, simply as *right*.

## V. Restraint as craft

The temptation in generative work is accumulation: more particles, more octaves, more passes.
Permeate rejects this. The system runs lean enough that a mid-range phone holds sixty frames
while the page scrolls, because a beautiful algorithm that stutters is not beautiful — it is
merely ambitious. Every element earns its cost. The particle count adapts to the device; the
field does not. The resolution adapts; the law does not. A visitor on a modest handset in
Galewela and a visitor on a workstation see the same physics, the same verdicts, the same
water — one simply sees it with fewer witnesses.

This is the final and most important instruction: the result must feel *engineered*, not
decorated. It should carry the quiet authority of an instrument built by someone who has spent
years on exactly this problem — every constant deliberate, every transition damped, every
threshold earned. Beauty here is a by-product of correctness, and the correctness is the point.

## Parameters of the system

| Parameter | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `seed` | Deterministic origin of the entire population |
| `flux` | Field velocity — how hard the system is driven |
| `recovery` | Fraction of particles that permeate (the threshold) |
| `polarisation` | Strength of concentration build-up at the membrane face |
| `octaves` | Curl-noise layers contributing to the flow |
| `witnesses` | Particle count — adapts to device, never to the physics |
| `caustic gain` | Power curve sharpening the wave interference |

## Conceptual seed

The reference is embedded, not announced: the transfiguration threshold sits at the recovery
ratio of a well-designed brackish-water RO train, the rejected stream leaves along the
tangential path of a real brine channel, and the bright meniscus that forms at the membrane
face is concentration polarisation rendered honestly. An engineer will recognise their plant.
Everyone else will simply see clean water being made.
