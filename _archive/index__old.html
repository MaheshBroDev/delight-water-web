About Us

Delight Water Solutions (Private) Limited, established in 2016, is dedicated to providing comprehensive solutions for water purification and waste water management projects. We offer a broad range of services, supplies, components, and accessories, as well as customized solutions such as reengineering, upgrading, and refurbishing existing plants. Our expertise extends to operation and maintenance solutions, chlorination, and the supply of spare parts and chemicals.

Initially focused on serving the household sector with drinking water solutions, Delight Water Solutions has expanded into industrial market segments to meet the growing demand for comprehensive water management services. We assist our clients in achieving optimal designs, utilizing the best technologies and operational techniques to fulfill their specific requirements.
